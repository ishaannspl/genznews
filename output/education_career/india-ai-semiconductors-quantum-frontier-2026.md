## The Breakdown

Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman recently called out foreign narratives suggesting India missed the AI revolution. Speaking at an IIT Madras alumni event, she pointed out that while domestic AI startups are scaling up or hitting unicorn status, many avoid public stock listings due to heavy bureaucratic compliance.

To bridge the gap, the government wants a heavy pivot toward building the actual physical infrastructure, hardware, and educational frameworks needed for deep tech. Beyond software, Sitharaman flagged semiconductors, quantum computing, and advanced defence manufacturing like drones as the country's next major economic battlegrounds.

## Why It Matters

For students and young professionals, this policy shift signals a massive job market pivot. Degrees and skill sets in hardware engineering, applied AI for manufacturing, and quantum tech are about to become some of the most leveraged assets in the country. If you want to future-proof your career, mastering these emerging tools right now is your best bet.
