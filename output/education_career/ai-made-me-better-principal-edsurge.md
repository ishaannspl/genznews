## The Hook

School administrators are drowning in endless paperwork and decision fatigue, but one principal found an unexpected fix: treating AI as a mental sounding board instead of just a faster typist.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- A Virginia high school principal uses AI voice features during her commute to talk through daily priorities and prepare for tough conversations.
- Instead of dumping time into typing emails, she uses custom AI tools to build workflows, organize school data, and convert dense memos into quick audio briefings.
- Strict privacy rules, like never inputting student or staff data, keep the tech safe while freeing up mental space for real human connection.

## The Breakdown

Monika Vereb, a principal in a large Virginia school district, argues that the real enemy in education leadership isn't a lack of time, but a shortage of uninterrupted focus. By using ChatGPT's voice feature during her morning drive, she turns her commute into a structured reflection space to prep for difficult meetings without bringing stress home.

Beyond commutes, she leverages tools like Gemini to transform heavy weekly memos into 90-second podcast-style audio updates for busy teachers. Rather than letting AI make final calls, she uses it to organize school improvement data, spot blind spots, and clear out administrative mental clutter.

## Why It Matters

Whether you work in education, tech, or corporate entry-level roles, digital burnout hits the exact same way. Using emerging tech to offload cognitive grunt work isn't about working faster just to do more tasks; it is about reclaiming the mental clarity needed to actually talk to humans and make smart choices.
